## &#x09;Superstore Sales

##### Project Overview

This project focuses on examining Superstore sales data to understand how the business performs. I looked at sales, profit, products, customers, regions, discounts, shipping, and customer segments to discover different patterns within the data. I first inspected the dataset using Excel. After that, I used MySQL and MySQL Workbench to write SQL queries and answer different questions from the data. Finally, I used Power BI to build a dashboard and present the results in a clearer and easier-to-understand way.



This is a beginner-level data analytics project that I developed to practice the process of working with data. This project gave me experience cleaning and checking data in Excel, analyzing it with SQL, and creating visualizations and a dashboard in Power BI.

##### Key Insights

* Total sales in the dataset are approximately **$2.30 million**, with total profit of about **$286.4K**.
* The **West** region has the highest sales, at approximately **$725.5K**, and also has the highest total profit.
* **Phones** are the highest-selling sub-category, with sales of about **$330K**.
* **Tables** have a negative total profit of approximately **-$17.7K**, making them one of the main loss-making sub-categories.
* **Sean Miller** is the customer with the highest total sales, at approximately **$25K**.
* The **Consumer** segment has the highest sales and profit compared with the Corporate and Home Office segments.
* Sales increased over the years in the dataset, with **2017** having the highest total sales at approximately **$733.2K**.
* The data also shows that higher discounts can have a negative effect on profit. The **30%+ discount** group has a negative average profit.
